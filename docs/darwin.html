Darwin Reel Editor

In development. Here's what's coming.

📜
Script-driven layout
Write a script in DOCX with [MM:SS] timestamps and /split start / /split end directives. The editor reads these and builds the visual timeline automatically.
🎭
Darwin mascot + stock footage
Upload one Darwin mascot video and your own stock clips. At each /split, the layout splits in half — stock on top, Darwin still talking on the bottom.
🎚️
Three-layer timeline
Independent control of video stack, voiceover, and background music — drag, trim, delete, reorder.
💬
Editable on-screen captions
Captions are pulled from your script. Edit text, change font family and size, drag to position. Adjustable per caption.
📐
Vertical 1080 × 1920 output
Built for shorts, reels, and stories. Same fast render pipeline as the testimonial editor.
